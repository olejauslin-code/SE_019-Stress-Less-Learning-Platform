About Us

Stress-Less is a tool for you and your family to learn and understand online safety. We teach how social media affects your brain and how to use it safely and productively.

Our Mission

Who are We?

Stress-Less was founded in Berlin by students at CODE University. We work with education experts to make our lessons safe, engaging, and accurate.

Our Goals

We help families use technology positively while teaching younger users how to protect their mental health and privacy online.
